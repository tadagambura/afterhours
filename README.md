# AfterHours

**Better questions for couples and friends**

> #3 of 15 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Weekly · Easy, content-heavy · Revenue potential: Medium–high · Social, Play & Style

## What it is

Swipeable question decks for first dates, couples, long-distance relationships, friends, family, road trips and parties. Optional 'answer privately, then reveal together' mode for couples.

## Why it's in the portfolio

A proven category with paid content packs and lots of social sharing. Packs are one-off purchases with high margins, and seasonal packs (Valentine's, Christmas) bring repeat sales. AfterHours's wheel and AfterHours's quizzes now live inside it, adding play and shareability at almost no cost.

## Who it's for

Couples, long-distance partners, friend groups, people who dislike small talk.

**The problem:** Conversations get stale; people want prompts that go deeper or get a group laughing.

## Version one

- Swipeable question cards
- Free starter decks: first date, couples, friends, party
- Favourites and shuffle
- Custom decks
- AfterHours wheel: spin for who goes first, a random question or a dare
- Compatibility quizzes with shareable couple and friend-group results

**Example:** A couple picks 'Date Night: 20 questions' and takes turns on the sofa; on Valentine's Day they buy the seasonal pack.

## Later

- Paid packs: date night, long distance, 18+ behind an age gate
- All-access subscription with a new pack each month
- Seasonal packs

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Full drama.** Duolingo-style theatrics: characters, big over-the-top reactions, sound effects. Thirsty's plant death is the only animation that takes over the full screen ('NOOOOOOO!!!! NOT CYPHICUS!!!!').

- Card flip with a dun-dun-DUN
- The spicy deck is literally on fire (extinguisher button)
- Next deck? The wheel spins and declares THE WHEEL HAS SPOKEN
- Answer privately, then reveal together
- Favourite: a heart pops and floats up

**Line we don't cross:** Playful, never embarrassing: reveals only happen when both people tap.

## AI

**Offline only.** AI helps draft question packs; a person curates them. No AI calls in the app.

## Watch-outs

Question quality is everything: AI can draft, but a human must curate. Adult packs need an age gate and app-store policy checks. Similar apps exist (for example Candle), so the edge is the quality of the experience and the marketing.
