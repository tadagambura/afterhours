# AfterHours: Spec

**Rank in portfolio:** #5 of 15 (easiest → hardest) · **Difficulty:** Easy (content-heavy)

## One-liner

Question decks for couples, friends, first dates and deep conversations.

## Problem / audience

- **Who it's for:** Couples, friends, people on first dates or in long-distance relationships, party hosts.
- **The problem it solves:** Conversations get stale; people want prompts that go deeper or get a group laughing.

## MVP features (ship with only these)

1. Swipeable question cards
2. 3–4 free decks (first dates, friends, deep talk, party)
3. Favourites
4. Random / shuffle mode
5. Share a question as an image

## Not in MVP

- Paid decks (date night, long distance, spicy 18+ behind age gate)
- Subscription for all decks + new monthly pack

## Monetisation

| Model | Detail |
|---|---|
| Free | Starter decks, with ads |
| Paid | Packs £1.99–£3.99 each, or £3.99/month for everything |
| Ads | Between decks only |

Prices are starting points from the portfolio brief; test before committing.

## Platform & stack

- **Platform:** Expo (web + iOS + Android).
- **Stack:** shared portfolio foundation (Expo, PostHog, RevenueCat, AdMob). See the portfolio roadmap.
- **Data:** Device only. Decks ship as content files.

## Growth engine hooks

- **Shareable output:** Question images ("Ask your partner this tonight").
- **Content pillars:** Questions to ask; Date-night ideas; Relationship check-ins; Party games

## Notes

Code is simple; the work is writing **good** questions (hundreds). AI can draft, a human must curate. Paid packs need in-app purchase (RevenueCat).
